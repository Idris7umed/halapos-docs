The HalaPOS dashboard shows your daily, monthly, and yearly sales, purchases, expenses, and payments.

![Dashboard](/images/light/dashboard.png 'HalaPOS - Dashboard')
![Dashboard](/images/dark/dashboard.png 'HalaPOS - Dashboard')

## Top bar

The app top bar displays the first character of your company name as a logo, an icon menu to open the point of sale screen, an add-new dropdown, and — on the right — menus for alerts, reports, settings, and user.

## Left side bar

HalaPOS has a left side bar that is fully displayed on large screens and collapsed on small screens.

## Main area

The dashboard main area shows the month's and year's sales, purchases, expenses, and payments totals, along with daily sales, purchases, expenses, and payments presented as a heatmap chart for the selected year.
